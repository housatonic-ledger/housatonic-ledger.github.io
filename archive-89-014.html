Record 89-014

A private photograph attributed to 1989, identified in the collection as James and Maria Harper.

Photographic index   |   Date attributed to 1989   |   Private collection

One print in a group of family photographs is identified on its accompanying catalogue slip as showing James and Maria Harper, longtime friends of Father Eddie Howard of Monroe.

The print carries no further description of the occasion. Neither the photographer nor the precise setting has been established.
