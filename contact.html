Contact / Newsroom

Get in touch with the Ledger.

Have a neighborhood story, a historic photograph, a community notice or a correction to share? The Ledger welcomes correspondence from across western Connecticut.

The editorial desk

We follow stories in Newtown, Monroe, Brookfield, Southbury and neighboring communities. For local history, features and community news, write to:

contact.housatonicledger@gmail.com

For corrections, please identify the article and the passage you believe needs attention. For archived photographs or correspondence, include the approximate date and town when known.

Our approach

The newsroom prioritizes local stories with clear public interest, firsthand recollections and verifiable records. Submitting an idea does not guarantee publication.

Newsroom correspondence is reviewed as editorial resources permit.

About the region

From historical societies to neighborhood gatherings, the stories of western Connecticut deserve a place in the local record. You can explore our coverage through the newspaper’s community, local history and archives sections.
