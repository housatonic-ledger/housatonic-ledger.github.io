Local History / 1973–1974

Regional Records

Recovered inspection note · Duplicated copy

Older construction records are among the most fragile parts of the county’s public memory. Their purpose was practical: a change of ownership, the removal of unsafe materials, an inspection before a family moved in. Over time, the paperwork has acquired another kind of importance.

The following record was transcribed from a partially preserved reconstruction file. The original annotations are not all legible.

NEW BUILDING INSPECTION · COUNTY FILE 73-041
LOCATION: NEWTOWN VICINITY
STATUS: RECONSTRUCTION / 1973—1974
FOUNDATION RETAINED: ████████████████
SUB-LEVEL: ████████
CHECKED BY: ████████████
INSPECTION COPY 02 / COPY 02 / COPY 02
STRUCTURE CLEARED ON: 04 / 19 / 1974
STRUCTURE CLEARED ON: ██ / ██ / 1974
FOUNDATION RETAINED: NO / YES / NO

The registry does not explain the difference between the copies. A second inventory has been requested from the records desk.
