The Ledger / From the desk

The stories of a place are never only its headlines.

From Newtown and Monroe to the communities along the Housatonic River, the Ledger follows the people, places and records that shape everyday life in western Connecticut.

We publish reported features, local-history notes, neighborhood voices and selections from the public record. Some subjects make the front page. Others survive in small boxes of photographs.
